04 — Editorial styling

Editorial Styling

A group project recreating six looks from Gucci’s Ancora campaign, Spring/Summer 2024, studying colour, silhouette, accessories and posing.

All projects

Overview The campaign The looks

The campaign

Ancora, also now, also then

The group researched Gucci’s campaigns and chose Ancora, Spring/Summer 2024. It was unveiled in January 2024, a few months after the runway show, and was Sabato De Sarno’s first advertising campaign as creative director.

The name plays on the Italian word’s double meaning of “again” and “still”, which Gucci framed as “also now, also then.” Six looks were styled as they appear, matching garments, accessories, proportions and attitude.

Saturated whites, deep burgundy, navy and light-wash denim, against a clean studio backdrop, with models posed close together, set the standard for the study.

Final looks

Six looks, studied in the studio

These photographs are from that styling study. The campaign’s whites, burgundy, navy and denim are the palette the group worked to recreate.
