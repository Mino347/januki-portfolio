03 — Global Crossroads

Cosmic Heirloom

Urban contemporary wear that brings Afrofuturism together with Mexican Otomi embroidery from Tenango. The collection is told through The Threadwalker’s Journey.

Heritage is carried into a futuristic identity, not stored as a relic.

Approach and concept

The Threadwalker’s Journey

Afrofuturism supplies cosmic imagery and a vision of the future. Tenango embroidery supplies dense, colourful hand-stitched patterns of animals, birds and flowers. Together they form a collection where heritage is carried forward.

The Threadwalker is a symbolic traveler moving through time and space, weaving ancestral wisdom into a future vision. Each chapter is a phase of awakening, from cultural roots and the echoes of the earth to signals sent into space. The garments become vessels of memory, resistance and transformation.

Heirloom means the traditions, stories and artistry passed down. Cosmic means that heritage does not belong to one time or place. A cosmic heirloom is rooted in the past and made for the stars.
